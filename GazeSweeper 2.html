THE LAW OF THE EYES

The front camera of the phone watches your eyes. Not the rear lens. Not a tap. The irises move the ring. A blink digs. A wink flags.

Open this file in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome. Allow Camera when asked. Prop the phone so the small FRONT CAMERA window shows both of your eyes. Hold the device still after that.

Look at a square until the ring turns blue. That is focus. A single blink is ignored — you still have to see. Double blink to dig. Wink left or hold a long blink to flag.

Nine red coins first: look, then blink. If you move the phone, calibrate again.

iPhone will not open the camera from a downloaded file, or from inside this chat. The page must run in Safari on a real website (https). A file in the Files app is not enough.
